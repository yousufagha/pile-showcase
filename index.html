How it works

From a pile of supplier paperwork to the status of every order, in four steps. "Walk me through it" shows the five things that matter.

  1. Read everythingPDFs, scans, phone photos, emails and their attachments, spreadsheets, Word files and Peppol e-invoices. Each file is read once; exact copies are recorded, not counted twice.
  2. Recognise and extractThe type comes from the printed title. Each value is taken only with evidence: printed next to its label, matched to the supplier list, or a structured e-invoice field. Totals, tax and tax-invoice rules are checked.
  3. Link and matchDeliveries, invoices, credits and payments are linked to their purchase order, then matched line by line: by item code, steel section or description, with units converted.
  4. Report and decideEach order's stage and what is owed: goods, paperwork, money. Anything that does not reconcile is an exception in plain words, with the money at stake.
The model reads pages; every match, sum and decision is ordinary codeNothing is guessed: no evidence, no valueEvery decision and rule change is kept with who and when

Exceptions

What the system could not settle by itself: a plain-language diagnosis with the money at stake, a suggested resolution, the PO, receipt and bill lines behind it, and the supplier's history where it repeats.
